Verification methodology & standards

VeritasLens operates under transparent, evidence-based standards modeled after the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) Code of Principles.

1. Core Principles of Verification

1

Primary source primacy

We prioritize direct public records, official gazettes, court filings, regulatory decisions, and peer-reviewed journals over secondary commentary.

2

Multi-source corroboration

Major assertions are checked across independent accredited wire desks (AP, Reuters, AFP) to avoid reliance on a single editorial perspective.

3

Transparent evidence chains

Every verdict identifies the specific evidence that supports or contradicts the claim, with permanent citation references so readers can verify the findings themselves.

4

Open corrections policy

If newly verified evidence emerges or archival records are updated, case files are amended transparently with versioned audit notes.

2. Standard Verdict Categories

To avoid ambiguous binary labels, VeritasLens applies five precise, standardized determinations based on documentary evidence:

VERIFIED AUTHENTIC Supported by Documentary Record

The claim is corroborated in all material respects by official gazettes, statutory registries, peer-reviewed clinical studies, or on-the-record accredited reporting with no substantiated contradictions.

FALSE / FABRICATED Contradicted or Forensically Disproven

The claim has no basis in the public record and is contradicted by direct primary evidence, official denial records, or forensic analysis proving synthetic generation (e.g., AI voice cloning).

MISATTRIBUTED CONTEXT Genuine Media, Incorrect Context

The imagery, audio, or quote is genuine but has been recycled from an unrelated event, past date, or different location, and mispresented as breaking news.

MISLEADING PRESENTATION Data Distortion or Visual Deception

The underlying numbers or citations exist, but have been selectively presented, mathematically exaggerated (such as truncated Y-axes), or taken out of their defining statistical scope.

UNVERIFIED / CONTEXT REQUIRED Insufficient Public Corroboration

Public documentary records do not contain adequate verifiable citations to substantiate or refute the claim. Readers are cautioned to await on-the-record primary statements.

3. Evidence Assessment & Weighting Criteria

When evaluating claims, evidence is weighted according to institutional accountability and verifiability:

  • Official Statutory Records: Formal government notices, patents, regulatory procedures, and court docket filings carry the highest evidentiary value because they represent binding legal actions.
  • Accredited News Agencies: Wire dispatches from organizations bound by the IFCN Code of Principles or equivalent standards are treated as verified secondary corroboration.
  • Forensic Artifacts: Audio frequency spectrograms, neural speech artifacts, and image keyframe reverse searches are treated as corroborating indicators, interpreted alongside documentary press schedules.
  • Social Media Dissemination: Viral metrics, engagement figures, and unverified social posts are treated solely as objects of evaluation, never as sources of truth.

4. Prototype Boundaries & Public Transparency

VeritasLens is an open evidence verification prototype created to demonstrate how modern newsrooms and researchers can structure claim audits:

  • Curated Demonstration Cases: The four reference cases included in VeritasLens represent real-world investigations grounded in actual regulatory decisions (EMA), government press pool logs (UK Cabinet Office), economic statistics (NSB), and conflict footage archives.
  • Custom Claims: Custom user queries are analyzed locally for linguistic markers and cross-referenced against demonstration reference templates. The system does not claim independent automated scraping of restricted, subscription-gated live wire services.
  • Local Browser Privacy: All session records and analysis history reside strictly within the client's local browser storage. No user search queries or personal data are transmitted to third-party ad networks or tracking servers.
