How VeritasLens works

A transparent, four-phase evidentiary workflow designed to audit viral statements against primary records, accredited wire archives, and forensic data.

Phase 1: Claim Ingestion & Proposition Decomposition

When a news headline or statement is submitted to VeritasLens, the system does not simply label the overall text as "real" or "fake." Viral misinformation frequently mixes genuine facts with deceptive extrapolations.

The ingestion process extracts discrete atomic claims. For example, if a post claims: “A leaked recording reveals the Prime Minister ordered a mandatory 40% energy cut during an emergency midnight session,” the statement is decomposed into three testable assertions:

  • Assertion A: Did the head of government issue an official energy rationing decree?
  • Assertion B: Did an emergency cabinet briefing take place on the alleged date?
  • Assertion C: Does the audio recording demonstrate genuine acoustic voice markers or neural synthesis artifacts?

Phase 2: Source Hierarchy & Primary Record Inspection

VeritasLens adheres to a strict evidentiary hierarchy. Sources are classified into distinct tiers based on legal enforceability, peer review, and journalistic accreditation:

T1

Tier 1: Official Registers & Gazettes

Direct public records, statutory instruments, regulatory approvals (e.g., EMA, FDA), court filings, and official government gazettes. Carries highest evidentiary weight.

T2

Tier 2: Accredited Wire Desks

Independent reporting from accredited international news agencies (Associated Press, Reuters, AFP) subject to strict fact-checking codes of ethics.

T3

Tier 3: Forensic & Archival Data

Acoustic spectrograms, neural speech model signatures, reverse image keyframe matches, and historical web archives with preserved hashes.

Phase 3: Weighing Corroboration & Documenting Disagreements

Rather than producing arbitrary percentage scores, VeritasLens assesses consensus qualitatively. The platform evaluates whether independent sources confirm, contextualize, or repudiate the claim.

Crucially, contradictory evidence is never hidden. If official spokespersons deny an allegation but investigative journalists uncover corroborating statutory filings, both findings are presented side-by-side in separate, readable columns:

  • Atomic Claim Breakdown: Line-by-line status (Corroborated, Refuted, or Uncorroborated) with exact findings.
  • Documented Sources & Records: Named organizations, publication dates, and specific reference catalog codes (e.g., EMEA/H/C/005821 or #CO-2026-092).

Phase 4: Audit Trails & Exportable Dossiers

Fact-checking must be reproducible. Every evaluation produces a standardized verification dossier indexed with a unique case ID (e.g., CASE-2026-0924). Users can:

  • Copy Summary Briefs: Formatted plain-text briefs ready for newsroom slack channels, editorial memos, or fact-checking databases.
  • Download Markdown Dossiers: Full, standalone .md documents containing all metadata, evidence tables, and IFCN compliance statements.
  • Audit Local Ledgers: All evaluated claims remain in your browser session for cross-case review and historical filtering.
