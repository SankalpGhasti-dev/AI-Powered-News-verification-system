CASE-2026-0924 · Geopolitics · Synthetic Media
FALSE / FABRICATED
Statement Evaluated:
“Leaked audio of Prime Minister discussing emergency energy rationing directives”

Investigative findings

Acoustic frequency analysis isolated harmonic voice-synthesis artifacts and unnatural phonetic cadence in the recording. Accredited press logs and official ministerial schedules confirm no security council briefing took place on the date alleged.

Atomic claim status

  • CLM-01 Refuted

    Prime Minister ordered mandatory 40% industrial energy cuts beginning November 1st.

    No executive decree or statutory instrument exists in the National Gazette or Energy Department archives.

  • CLM-02 Refuted

    The recording captures an emergency council briefing on energy reserves.

    Cabinet Office press rosters confirm accredited correspondents were present at regular briefing; no emergency session occurred.

Primary sources cited

Cabinet Office Press Statement Official Government Statement

On-the-record denial verifying prime ministerial schedule and confirming recording is counterfeit.

Official Record #CO-2026-092
Reuters Fact Check Wire Wire Service Fact-Check

Acoustic forensic analysis identified vocal synthesis models matching commercial neural voice clone signatures.

CASE-2026-0618 · Economics · Chart Manipulation
MISLEADING PRESENTATION
Statement Evaluated:
“Infographic alleging Central Bank confirmed 35% single-month inflation surge”

Investigative findings

The infographic truncates the vertical Y-axis (spanning exclusively 30% to 36%) to simulate dramatic volatility and isolates an annualized spot wholesale energy component, falsely presenting it as headline consumer price inflation.

Atomic claim status

  • CLM-01 Refuted

    Official monthly headline consumer inflation hit 35% in recent reporting window.

    National Statistics Bureau confirmed headline CPI stands at 3.2% year-on-year; 35% figure represents spot gas price volatility.

Primary sources cited

National Statistics Bureau Bulletin Official National Statistics

Official Statistical Gazette confirmed monthly headline CPI stood at 0.3% and 3.2% YoY.
