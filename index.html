How VeritasLens verifies claims

Our multi-stage verification pipeline separates emotional rhetoric from verifiable facts.

1

Deconstruct the assertion

Statements are decomposed into atomic, falsifiable propositions—stripping out sensational framing, emotive adjectives, and speculative conjecture.

2

Cross-reference primary records

Claims are compared directly against official government gazettes, regulatory filings, corporate registers, peer-reviewed studies, and accredited wire archives.

3

Weigh consensus & gaps

Every determination measures independent source agreement, documents explicit contradictory evidence, and preserves permanent citation references.

Evidence transparency standards

Trust is built by showing the underlying evidence, not by asserting authority.

Traceable Primary Citations

Every finding references a verifiable source identifier—statutory gazettes, regulatory approval references, or wire dispatch IDs—so anyone can inspect the raw source.

Explicit Counter-Evidence

We do not bury contradictory information. If reputable sources disagree or official denials conflict with forensic indicators, both are explicitly presented in separate sections.

Auditable Local Session Ledger

Checked claims are stored strictly in your browser's private local storage. VeritasLens does not send your queries to external profiling networks or sell reading habits.
