How VeritasLens works

A transparent, four-phase evidentiary pipeline designed to audit news articles and viral claims against live news wires, accredited fact-checking archives, and mathematical corroboration metrics.

Phase 1: Input Ingestion & HTML Article Extraction

Users can submit either a news article URL (HTTP/HTTPS) or paste statement text directly. For URLs, the backend applies rigorous SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) protections—blocking private, loopback, and cloud metadata IP ranges.

The backend fetches the raw web page with strict timeout controls and uses BeautifulSoup4 to strip away ads, headers, navigation chrome, and tracking scripts, isolating the core headline, author, publication date, and body paragraphs.

Phase 2: Atomic Claim Decomposition via Google Gemini

Complex articles often interleave verifiable factual statements with subjective rhetoric and opinion. VeritasLens passes the cleaned text to Google Gemini with strict structured schemas to extract discrete, falsifiable atomic claims.

For example, if an article states: “The Central Bank confirmed a catastrophic 35% single-month spike in headline inflation following emergency rate deliberations,” the engine extracts testable components:

  • Claim 1: The Central Bank officially reported a 35% month-over-month inflation increase.
  • Claim 2: Emergency monetary policy meetings were held by monetary authorities.

Phase 3: Multi-Source Evidence Retrieval

Once atomic claims are identified, VeritasLens gathers independent empirical evidence through two complementary external data sources:

E1

GNews Wire API

Searches accredited global news publishers and wire outlets for reporting that corroborates, contextualizes, or refutes each atomic claim.

E2

Google Fact Check Tools API

Queries existing verified fact-checks published by independent, audited news organizations indexed by Google's ClaimReview database.

E3

Evidence Deduplication

Retrieved records are cleaned, deduplicated, and organized by publisher, snippet, relevance, and original safe URL references.

Phase 4: Claim Verification, Credibility Scoring & Synthesis

Retrieved evidence is evaluated against the atomic claims by Google Gemini under strict evidentiary guidelines. The engine assigns each claim one of four explicit verdicts:

  • Supported: Substantive, reputable evidence independently confirms the core assertion.
  • Contradicted: Documented data, official records, or authoritative wire reports directly disprove the assertion.
  • Mixed: The assertion contains a kernel of truth blended with misleading, exaggerated, or outdated context.
  • Insufficient Evidence: No credible, authoritative corroboration was retrieved from news wires or fact-check records.

Based on claim-level verdicts and evidence coverage, the engine calculates a transparent 0–100 Credibility Score:

Score = 50.0 + (VerdictBalance × 45.0 × CoverageFactor)

Crucially, claims with Insufficient Evidence maintain a neutral baseline score of 50.0 rather than dropping to 0, preventing unverified rumors from being falsely labelled as definitively disproven.

Transparent Limitations & Reproducible Reports

Every verification report explicitly lists evidentiary limitations—including potential paywall restrictions, lack of regional news coverage, or breaking news latency. Users can copy summary briefs or download complete markdown reports for editorial documentation.
