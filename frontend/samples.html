CASE-2026-0618 · Economics · Metric Exaggeration
CONTRADICTED
Statement Evaluated:
“Viral infographic claims Central Bank confirmed a 35% single-month headline inflation surge”

Investigative findings & score: 18 / 100

Official statistical releases reported monthly CPI at 3.2% annualized. The viral graphic isolated a volatile wholesale spot gas sub-index and falsely presented it as aggregate headline consumer inflation.

Atomic claim status

  • CLM-01 Contradicted

    Central Bank confirmed headline consumer inflation surged by 35% in a single month.

    National Statistics Bureau reports aggregate headline CPI at 0.3% month-on-month and 3.2% annualized.

Primary sources cited

National Statistics Bureau Bulletin Official National Statistics

Official Statistical Gazette confirmed monthly headline CPI stood at 0.3% and 3.2% YoY.

CASE-2026-0812 · Infrastructure · Capital Funding
MIXED
Statement Evaluated:
“Government fully cancels the high-speed rail line project due to budget overruns”

Investigative findings & score: 52 / 100

The headline oversimplifies policy decisions. While the northern extension was cancelled and restructured, Phase 1 civil engineering and core corridor construction remain fully funded and under active construction.

Atomic claim status

  • CLM-01 Supported

    Government cancelled northern railway segments due to severe budget overruns.

    Department for Transport confirmed cancellation of secondary phases to cap expenditure.

  • CLM-02 Contradicted

    The entire high-speed rail project has been terminated.

    Primary line works and tunneling operations between major terminals remain actively underway.

Primary sources cited

Department for Transport Ministerial Statement Official Government Gazette

Policy statement detailing restructuring of high-speed rail expenditure program.

INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
