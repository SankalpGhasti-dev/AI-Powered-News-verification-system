About VeritasLens

We believe news verification is not just about debunking falsehoods, but about equipping journalists and readers with structured evidence and verifiable sources.

Our automated verification framework deconstructs complex news articles into atomic claims, cross-examines them against live GNews wire dispatches and Google Fact Check archives, and calculates transparent corroboration scores with documented limitations.

Open Investigation Framework

How VeritasLens verifies claims

Our multi-stage verification pipeline separates emotional rhetoric from verifiable facts.

1

Deconstruct into atomic claims

Google Gemini decomposes complex news text into distinct, falsifiable propositions—stripping away sensational framing and subjective adjectives.

2

Retrieve multi-source evidence

Claims are matched against live news wire dispatches via GNews and public fact-checking records via Google Fact Check Tools API.

3

Score & synthesize transparently

The system assigns one of four explicit verdicts, calculates a mathematical 0–100 credibility score, and details evidentiary limitations.

Evidence transparency standards

Trust is built by showing the underlying evidence, not by asserting authority.

Traceable Wire Citations

Every finding references verifiable source URLs from accredited news wires and fact-checking organizations, enabling users to verify findings independently.

Explicit Counter-Evidence & Gaps

We do not bury contradictory information. If reputable sources disagree or insufficient evidence is located, both aspects are presented prominently.

Auditable Local Session Ledger

Checked claims are logged locally in your browser's localStorage. Analysis requests are processed on the VeritasLens server and never sold or shared.
