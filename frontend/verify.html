    Our verification methodology

    VeritasLens follows four rigorous evidentiary principles: automated atomic claim decomposition, multi-source wire retrieval, mathematical corroboration scoring, and explicit limitation boundaries.

    1

    Atomic claim decomposition

    Google Gemini decomposes complex articles and viral headlines into falsifiable, testable atomic claims, stripping away subjective hyperbole.

    2

    Multi-source wire retrieval

    Claims are queried across accredited news wires via GNews and existing fact-check databases via Google Fact Check Tools API.

    3

    Transparent 0–100 scoring

    Credibility scores reflect evidence balance and coverage. A lack of evidence yields a neutral 50 score rather than an ungrounded refutation.

    4

    Explicit evidentiary limitations

    Every report details source gaps, paywall exclusions, and retrieval limits, ensuring users know what could and could not be verified.
